🌿 About TactileTrail

Philosophy: Touch Grass, Drop the Screen

Most nature apps force you to look at a glowing rectangle outdoors—identifying plants through camera viewfinders. TactileTrail flips the paradigm: put your phone in your pocket, put earbuds on, and let an intimate, David Attenborough-inspired naturalist speak directly into your ears.

Architecture & Integrations

  • Core AI Model: Google Gemma (via Google AI Studio & Hugging Face). Prompt-engineered with intimate botanical and bioacoustic knowledge.
  • Edge-AI Resilience: Built-in on-device Gemma simulation allowing full functionality without cell signal on remote mountain trails.
  • Audio Engine: ElevenLabs Text-to-Speech API streaming realistic, calming voice guidance, with graceful fallback to browser speech synthesis.
  • Sensory Soundscape: Web Audio API procedural synthesis of wind currents and birdsong.
  • Earbuds & Lockscreen: HTML5 MediaSession API allowing pocket playback controls via headphone buttons.
